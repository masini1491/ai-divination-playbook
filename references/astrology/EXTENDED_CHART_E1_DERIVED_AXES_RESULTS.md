## 5. House-system semantic boundary

This is the most important semantic constraint found in E1.

### Placidus

In the current Placidus house implementation, opposite cusps are generated by adding 180° to the corresponding parent cusp. Therefore:

```text
Descendant == Placidus house 7 cusp
Imum Coeli == Placidus house 4 cusp
```

for the current admitted Placidus geometry.

### Whole Sign

In Whole Sign houses, house cusps are sign boundaries derived from the Ascendant's sign, not the exact angular positions of DSC / IC.

Therefore in Whole Sign:

```text
Descendant != necessarily house 7 cusp
Imum Coeli != necessarily house 4 cusp
```

The exact angle facts remain:

```text
Descendant = Ascendant + 180°
Imum Coeli = Midheaven + 180°
```

independent of the selected house system.

A future implementation must therefore model angles and house cusps as distinct fact families. It must not create DSC or IC by reading Whole Sign cusp 7 / cusp 4.
