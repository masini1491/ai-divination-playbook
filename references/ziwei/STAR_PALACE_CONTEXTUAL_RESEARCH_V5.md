1. the semantic distinction is actually tied to the exact star×palace pair;

   favorable/adverse outcome language;

## Cell decisions

### 紫微 × 官祿宮 — KEEP REJECT-REDUNDANT

The targeted recheck did not find a stable mechanism materially beyond the
current admitted composition:

- 紫微 core already owns centrality / authority / coordination / leadership;
- 紫微 conditional evidence already makes strong authority expression dependent
  on supporting/adverse conditions;
- 官祿宮 already owns career, office, achievement, position and public
  responsibility;
- historical pair wording remains dominated by status / office elevation and
  support-condition language.

The exact pair is highly intuitive and may feel more specific in prose, but the
safe reusable information still collapses to the admitted components.

Routing remains:

`BOUNDED_L5_COMPOSITION / resolved / REJECT-REDUNDANT`

Revisit trigger: genuinely new pair-specific process/trajectory evidence, not
another restatement of office, rank, leadership or supportive-star strength.

### 天府 × 財帛宮 — KEEP REJECT-REDUNDANT

The targeted recheck again finds very high semantic overlap:

- 天府 core already owns storehouse, resource conservation, property/wealth and
  orderly retention;
- 財帛宮 already owns acquisition, accumulation and retention;
- historical and modern wording commonly repeats storage, saving, stable
  resource handling or favorable wealth language.

After safety normalization, the candidate still does not add a distinct process
that cannot be produced by bounded composition.

Routing remains:

`BOUNDED_L5_COMPOSITION / resolved / REJECT-REDUNDANT`

Revisit trigger: evidence for a stable pair-specific resource trajectory that is
not equivalent to "storehouse star in wealth domain" and does not depend on
guaranteed wealth outcomes.

### 七殺 × 福德宮 — REOPEN AS DEFERRED_EVIDENCE

V4 previously treated historical unease as fully representable by 七殺 intensity
plus 福德 inner-wellbeing semantics. The targeted recheck finds a narrower
candidate distinction worth preserving for further evidence review:

> the pair may describe an inner-wellbeing pattern in which rest/ease is less
> naturally static, while sustained purposeful engagement or activity can become
> part of how internal order is maintained.

This is more specific than merely saying "七殺 is intense" and "福德 concerns
inner ease", because the candidate concerns the *mode of regulation* inside the
福德 domain: ease versus continued activation is not treated as a simple
positive/negative scale.

Research-normalized candidate:

> 七殺落福德宮時，可研究為「內在安定較不偏純靜態休息，較可能透過持續投入、
> 目標性活動或保持行動感來維持內在秩序」的 contextual modifier；實際表現仍
> 高度依賴廟陷、吉煞與同會條件，不得翻成必然勞苦、工作成癮、心理疾病、
> 固定幸福程度或具體人生事件。

Why this is not production-ready:

- the distinction is still partly reconstructed from historical unease wording
  plus recurring modern paraphrase;
- exact condition boundaries are not yet isolated;
- the current 七殺 conditional claim requires richer dignity / auxiliary /
  star-relation facts than default natal synthesis necessarily exposes;
- a production claim would need a separate source/admission review proving that
  the pair-specific regulation mechanism is stable and safely renderable.

New research routing:

`DEFERRED_EVIDENCE / reviewed / unresolved / ADMISSION-CANDIDATE-RECHECK`

This is a research reopening only. The production registry remains unchanged.
