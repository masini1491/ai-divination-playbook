### 天梁 × 父母宮 — REOPEN AS DEFERRED_EVIDENCE

V4 correctly observed that 天梁 already owns elder/protection semantics and 父母宮 is the parent domain. The targeted recheck, however, finds a narrower interaction candidate that is not fully captured by merely saying "protective elder in parent domain":

> sustained parental protection / guidance may itself become a strong organizing relationship pattern, making the native's autonomy / separation boundary more salient.

The key distinction is not "parents are good" or "parents protect the native". It is the pair-level *relationship dynamic* between protection, guidance and autonomy.

Research-normalized candidate:

> 天梁落父母宮時，可研究為「長輩／父母的照顧、規範與介入感較可能成為關係中的主要組織力量，因此自主決策與分離界線會更需要被看見」的 contextual modifier；不得翻成父母必然控制、命主必然依賴、固定家庭角色、父母健康／壽命或具體家庭事件。

Why this is not production-ready:

- much of the modern wording is practitioner-level;
- the exact boundary between ordinary 天梁 protection semantics and a distinct autonomy/separation mechanism still needs stronger independent evidence;
- condition handling for supportive vs intrusive expression is not isolated.
