## Scope differences preserved

### CG-FUDE-SCOPE-001

Historical 福德 core centers 福厚／享福／安樂與勞心不寧. Nihai extends 福德 strongly into spouse relationship and broad life-fortune synthesis. Resolution: `PRESERVE_SCOPE_DIFFERENCE`.

### CG-NUPU-SCOPE-001

Historical 奴僕 focuses servant/subordinate/support relationships. Modern usage often broadens it to friends, peers, collaborators and partners. Resolution: `PRESERVE_SCOPE_DIFFERENCE`.

## Safety

疾厄 and 父母 historical sources contain health, death, loss and kinship strong assertions. Those may remain source-bounded historical evidence but require pre-render safety gating and cannot become deterministic medical/death predictions.
