## Why a 168-cell dictionary is not the target

The current corpus already admits 14 major-star first-layer claims and 12 palace first-layer claims. Expanding every major star into every palace would create 168 cells even when no source establishes a materially distinct star-in-palace doctrine. That would duplicate the same star core and palace domain repeatedly and recreate the `flat_dictionary` failure mode rejected by the interpretation architecture.

The research target is therefore **composition with provenance**, not exhaustive cell filling.

## Composition rule

For an admitted chart fact such as `star_present:X` in `palace:Y`, the research synthesizer may place these independently admitted evidence nodes in the same frame:

```text
star core / star conditional
+
palace domain / palace conditional
```

This is an L5 bounded composition operation. It does **not** create a new L4 sourced claim and must not be rendered as though one source directly stated the combined sentence.

A dedicated `star×palace` L4 claim is justified only when at least one of the following is true:

1. a claim-eligible source explicitly makes a materially distinct star-in-palace assertion;
2. a named-tradition rule is separately admitted with its tradition/profile scope;
3. a project composition rule is explicitly admitted as `project_adoption` and clearly identified as synthesis policy rather than historical doctrine.

## Specificity and fallback

Within one authority chain:

```text
exact conditional combination
> source-explicit star×palace override
> star conditional
> star core
> palace conditional/domain
```

If no dedicated combination override exists, retrieval may return the admitted first-layer evidence separately and L5 may compose it. Missing override coverage is **not** permission for LLM-memory doctrine fill.

## Claim-family admission policy

Future combination batches should be **evidence-driven and sparse**:

- group claims by material semantic override, not by filling a Cartesian matrix;
- retain star/palace first-layer claims as separate provenance nodes;
- require explicit source locator for every dedicated L4 override;
- preserve tradition conflicts instead of averaging;
- reject a combination record when it merely restates star core + palace domain without material extra semantics.
