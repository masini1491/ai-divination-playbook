# Zi Wei Scope A Production v1 Admission

Status: **PRODUCTION ADMITTED — EXPLICIT REQUEST ONLY / G8 ORDINARY ROUTING NOT ADMITTED**

## Decision

Production v1 admits only the already-selected Scope A:

- natal baseline only;
- deterministic Scope-A natal provider;
- 14 major-star + 12 palace first-layer corpus (52 admitted claims);
- deterministic retrieval/composition;
- registered-conflict preservation;
- uncertainty/safety delivery actions.

Production authority is granted by `ZIWEI_PRODUCTION_ADMISSION_V1.json` plus `tools/ziwei_scope_a_pipeline.py`. Research registries remain historically `production_routable=false`; they are allowlisted by the production manifest rather than rewritten.

## Input boundary

The production pipeline accepts already-normalized traditional-lunar facts. Gregorian→lunisolar conversion and leap-month normalization are not admitted by this Stage. Calendar provenance remains required.

## Explicit exclusions

No production authority is granted for brightness-conditioned interpretation, auxiliary/minor stars, Four Transformations, Cartesian star×palace claims, decadal/yearly/monthly/daily/hourly layers, or dynamic prediction.

The pipeline grants no final-prose authority and makes no scientific or predictive-validity claim.

## Routing boundary

G7 admission does not perform G8 ordinary routing. `METHOD_ROUTING.md`, `ZIWEI.md` and machine routing remain a separate next gate.
